Cube recipes

Corruption

Put an item and an Orb of Corruption in the cube and Transmute twice. The first press rolls the corruption (the orb comes back); the second uses the orb up and applies one result from the table. A corrupted item can't be corrupted again. 'Item turns Rare/Magic' means a unique, set or rare item loses its quality - the risk of corrupting.
