Renegade calculator

How tough a Renegade is where you meet it: its life, damage and skill level, from the same numbers the game uses.

RenegadeLifeDamageSkill levelCasts and leaps

A Renegade has the monster level of the area it turns up in (a terror zone raises it). Its gear adds on top: +skills raise its skill level, and its resistances, speed, crushing blow, life leech and enhanced damage count for a quarter in early areas and in full from monster level 80 - so a well-geared one is tougher than this table. Damage is its bonus over the base hit; casts and leaps: how much slower than at the top of the ramp it acts.
