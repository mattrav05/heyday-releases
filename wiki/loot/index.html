Loot Bags

Cube one by itself to open it (the Heyday Loot Bags plugin rolls this table with the game's drop code).

  • Drops: maps 1 in 150 / 1 in 15 / 33% (regular / champion / unique), Hell's Torment 1 in 500 / 1 in 50, the rips 1 in 700 / 1 in 70
  • 3 rolls per bag, each:
    • nothing: 31.2%
    • junk gear (Act 5 / Act 1 Hell): 26.0%
    • potions (super, Experience, Magic Find, Sunder): 15.6%
    • a jewel (magic 12 : rare 5 : unique 1): 8.3%
    • Soul Shard / Gem Cluster / Pandemonium Key / Standard / Torment key: 8.3%
    • an orb (not map orbs): 5.2%
    • runes (1 in 13 a high rune, Mal-Cham): 4.2%
    • a unique ring or amulet: 0.8%
    • an Ancient Statue: 0.4%
  • Per bag: unique jewel 1 in 70, unique ring or amulet 1 in 40, high rune 1 in 44, Ancient Statue 1 in 80

Hoardlings

Every map holds one (two or three with the of the Hoard / of Plunder map affixes); Hell's Torment and the rips 1 in 4,000 per kill. Each tenth of its life lost drops one drip roll; killing it drops the burst; it escapes 40 seconds after the first hit.

  • Gilded: burst 14 rolls of gld 8, Act 5 (H) Uitem C 1; drip: gld 1
  • Gem: burst 10 rolls of 1gc 3, gpv 1, gpb 1, gpy 1, gpr 1, gpg 1, gpw 1, skz 1; drip: 1gc 1, gpv 1, gpb 1, gpy 1, gpr 1, gpg 1, gpw 1, skz 1
  • Rune: burst 8 rolls of Runes 17 10, Heyday Bag High Rune 1; drip: Runes 17 1
  • Bag: burst 6 rolls of lbg 1; drip: lbg 1
  • Cartographer: burst 6 rolls of Heyday Maps 1, Heyday Map Orbs 1; drip: Heyday Map Orbs 2, Heyday Maps T1 1
  • Soul: burst 10 rolls of sfg 1; drip: sfg 1
  • Jewel: burst 8 rolls of Heyday Bag Magic Jewel 40, Heyday Bag Rare Jewel 10, Heyday Torment Unique Jewel 1; drip: Heyday Bag Magic Jewel 5, Heyday Bag Rare Jewel 1
  • Charm: burst 6 rolls of Heyday Hoard Unique Charm 1, Heyday Hoard Charms 2; drip: Heyday Hoard Charms 9, Heyday Hoard Unique Charm 1
  • Jewel Hoardling: a unique jewel on 17% of kills (only in the burst)
  • Charm Hoardling: about 2.9 unique charms per full kill (burst + drips); its level is capped so Annihilus and the class Hellfire Torches can't roll
