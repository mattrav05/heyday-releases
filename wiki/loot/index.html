Hoardlings

Every map holds one (two or three with the of the Hoard / of Plunder map affixes); Hell's Torment and the rips 1 in 4,000 per kill. Each tenth of its life lost drops one drip roll; killing it drops the burst; it escapes 40 seconds after the first hit.

  • Gilded: burst 14 rolls of gld 8, Act 5 (H) Uitem C 1; drip: gld 1
  • Gem: burst 10 rolls of 1gc 3, gpv 1, gpb 1, gpy 1, gpr 1, gpg 1, gpw 1, skz 1; drip: 1gc 1, gpv 1, gpb 1, gpy 1, gpr 1, gpg 1, gpw 1, skz 1
  • Rune: burst 8 rolls of Runes 17 10, Heyday Bag High Rune 1; drip: Runes 17 1
  • Bag: burst 6 rolls of lbg 1; drip: lbg 1
  • Cartographer: burst 6 rolls of Heyday Maps 1, Heyday Map Orbs 1; drip: Heyday Map Orbs 2, Heyday Maps T1 1
  • Soul: burst 10 rolls of sfg 1; drip: sfg 1
  • Jewel: burst 8 rolls of Heyday Bag Magic Jewel 40, Heyday Bag Rare Jewel 10, Heyday Torment Unique Jewel 1; drip: Heyday Bag Magic Jewel 5, Heyday Bag Rare Jewel 1
