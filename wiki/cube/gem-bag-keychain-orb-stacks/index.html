Gem Bag, Keychain & orb stacks

The Gem Bag stores gems as credits. Many recipes spend credits instead of gems - shown as 'Gem Bag (needs N gems)'. A Grabber takes one gem back out. The Keychain does the same for keys, and orbs stack like runes.
