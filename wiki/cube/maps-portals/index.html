Maps & portals

A map or key transmuted in Harrogath (Act 5 town, any difficulty) opens a red portal next to you. Three maps of a tier make one of the next tier. Map orbs work on a map anywhere: Ascension raises it a tier, Alteration switches its zone (Ruined Cathedral / Chaos Spire), Duplication makes a second copy. Orbs drop inside maps (champions 1 in 120, unique monsters 1 in 12, any monster 1 in 1,200), and in Hell's Torment and Hell terror zones (Ascension and Alteration only). They stack in the Maps tab of the stash; Ctrl+click or Shift+click sends a map there from any stash tab. Dropped maps show a beam of light; names are coloured by tier (yellow, orange, red). The Loot Filter has a Maps group (Items > Misc > Maps). Maps are always terrorized, and Heralds can appear in maps and in Hell's Torment.
