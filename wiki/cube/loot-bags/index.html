Loot Bags

A Loot Bag in the cube by itself opens on Transmute and its loot falls at your feet: three rolls, each nothing, junk gear, potions, a jewel, Soul Shards / Gem Clusters / keys / a Standard, an orb, runes, a unique ring or amulet, or an Ancient Statue. Per bag: unique ring or amulet 1 in 40, unique jewel 1 in 70, high rune 1 in 44. Bags drop in the maps (1 in 150 / 1 in 15 / 33%), Hell's Torment (1 in 500 / 1 in 50) and the rips (1 in 700 / 1 in 70). Full odds: the Loot page.
