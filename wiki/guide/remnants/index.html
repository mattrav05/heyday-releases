Remnants

Sometimes the fallen leave something behind: a shrine-like stone with a gift for whoever touches it first.

On this pageWhere they show upThe three kindsBuff icons

Where they show up

  • In Hell's Torment, the maps, the rips and Hell's terror zones, every kill has a 1 in 800 chance to leave a Remnant where the monster fell.
  • A map with the of the Fallen or of the Restless Dead affix leaves many more.
  • Each Remnant gives its gift once, to whoever touches it first.

The three kinds

  • Remnant of Fortune (25% of them): +100% magic find or +200% gold find for 2 minutes - it stacks with everything else, and a second one refreshes the time - or loot spills out of it: Abundance (a top-tier map unique's loot, twice), 3 runes or 4 perfect gems, skulls and gem clusters.
  • Remnant of War (50%): a champion of the fallen rises - a boss version of the monster that dropped it - a Herald of Terror answers with its group, or the Gauntlet begins: 3 waves of 8 of the fallen's kind around you, all to be cleared within 45 seconds. Clear them in time and you get both Fortune gifts and a Hoardling; too slow and the rest stay to fight for nothing. Their loot is yours to take.
  • Remnant of Greed (25%): a Hoardling appears. Catch it!

Buff icons

Your buffs show as icons with a countdown just above the skill bar - Battle Orders, Shout, armors, shrines and the Remnants' gifts: Find Item's icon for the magic find, Find Potion's for the gold find and War Cry's for a Gauntlet in progress.
