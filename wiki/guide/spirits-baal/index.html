Spirits & Baal

Spirit of Andariel: Worldstone Keep level 2. Spirit of Duriel: Throne of Destruction. Spirits of Diablo and Mephisto: Worldstone Chamber.

Every Spirit and every Hell Baal kill rolls 4 items (10 while Act 5 is terrorized) with improved unique and set odds, plus the boss bonus table and a 75% chance of a Soul Shard. Duriel's Spirit has a 50% chance of an Orb of Infusion, Mephisto's of an Orb of Assemblage, Diablo's of an Orb of Conversion (a sure drop while terrorized).

Hell's Torment Portal Key: 1 in 24 from Baal and from the Spirits of Diablo and Mephisto.
