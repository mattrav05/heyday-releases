The Rips (Hell 4-8)

Kill the Guardian Angel at the Arreat Summit in Hell for a Guardian Angel's Lair Key.

Tome of Town Portal + Guardian Angel's Lair Key + a rune opens a rip: El = Hell 4, Eld = Hell 5, Tir = Hell 6, Nef = Hell 7, Eth = Hell 8.

Rip monsters have 90% resistance to everything and give far more experience, and each rip is harder than the last: monster level 98, 103, 108, 113, 118. Bring all the minus-resistance you have.
