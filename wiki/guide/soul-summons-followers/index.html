Soul Summons (Followers)

Class followers you summon from the Beacon of Hope and level up with Soul Shards and Class Tokens.

On this pageGetting startedCube recipesTiersWhat each one casts

Getting started

  • The Beacon of Hope drops from your first Normal Baal kill.
  • Soul Shards drop from the Spirits and Baal (75% chance, more while terrorized) and the Three (about 2.5 per kill). They stack in their own slot on the stash's Materials tab (ctrl+click files them there).

Cube recipes

  • Beacon + Soul Shards: adds 1-3 Soul Shards per shard to the Beacon.
  • Beacon alone (200+ Soul Shards): a Class Token. Cube a token alone to change its class.
  • Beacon + 1 Class Token: unlock your first Soul. It appears as an oskill on the Beacon.
  • Beacon + N tokens of that class: upgrade that Soul from tier N-1 to tier N, one tier at a time - tier 2 takes 2 tokens, then tier 3 takes 3 more, tier 4 another 4, up to 10 for tier 10. Saving tokens up doesn't skip tiers: 8 tokens on a tier 2 Soul does nothing; put in exactly the next tier's number.
  • One token of each class: an Expansion Token. Beacon + Class Token + Expansion Token = unlock another Soul.

Tiers

Every skill of a follower is 3 levels per tier, so a tier 10 follower casts everything at level 30 - Heyday's skill cap. Their gear grows with the tier too, from plain elite bases to rare and unique pieces, the best on tier 10.

What each one casts

  • Amazon: Charged Strike, Lightning Strike, Lightning Fury, Charge, Inner Sight, Plague Javelin, Penetrate. Tier 10 wears unique Hyperion Javelin, Sacred Armor.
  • Sorceress: Frozen Orb, Fire Ball, Shiver Armor, Chain Lightning, Thunder Storm, Blizzard, Meteor. Tier 10 wears unique Grim Shield, Sacred Armor, Sparkling Ball.
  • Necromancer: Bone Spear, Poison Nova, Bone Armor, Teeth, Bone Prison, Life Tap, Weaken. Tier 10 wears unique Sacred Armor, Unearthed Wand.
  • Paladin: Blessed Hammer, Shield Throw, Holy Shield, Sacrifice, Fist of the Heavens, Charge. Tier 10 wears unique Monarch, Phase Blade, Sacred Armor.
  • Barbarian: Chasm Break, Shout, Charge, Double Swing, Bash, Whirlwind. Tier 10 wears unique Champion Axe, Conqueror Crown, Sacred Armor.
  • Druid: Eruption, Cyclone Armor, Volcano, Twister, Tornado, Molten Boulder. Tier 10 wears unique Sacred Armor, Totemic Mask, Tyrant Club.
  • Assassin: Charged Bolt Sentry, Flameburst Sentry, Blade Shield, Fade, Quickness, Glacial Burst Sentry, Shock Field. Tier 10 wears unique Runic Talons, Scarab Husk, Scissors Suwayyah.
