Sieges

Build a castle in Harrogath, raise an army and lead it into an endless map of sieges. Your troops hold the line; you break the enemy.

On this pageSuppliesYour castleThe siege mapLaying siegeThe warlordTogether

Supplies

  • Timber (Act 1), Sandstone (Act 2), Witch-Resin (Act 3) and Hellforged Iron (Act 4) drop only in that act's terror zones, in Hell.
  • A kill there drops supplies 1 in 120 of the time (1 in 12 for a champion, 25% for a unique), 1-3 at once.
  • The Paragon Supplies stat raises the chance on your kills.
  • Quartermaster Hoardlings carry every act's supplies, and a Remnant of Fortune can spill them (Provisions) - both wherever they appear.
  • Siege warlords drop a big batch of supplies.

Your castle

  • The Castle Ledger stands in the yard beside the Barracks house in Harrogath (a red cross on the map). Deposit gold into its treasury (it never comes back out) and supplies into its stores. The ledger's View button shows your castle on its hill: every building you raise appears there, and grows from a timber hut to a stone hall as you upgrade it.
  • Buildings train your troops: the War Lodge (Barbarian Militia), Archery Range (Rogue Archers), Spear Yard (Desert Spears), Wolf Tower (Iron Wolves) and Siege Works (Siege Engines). Upgrading them costs gold and supplies; the Keep raises how far they can go.
  • Your army costs gold every hour, around the clock - even while you're away. With the treasury empty training stops, and after 12 hours your troops start deserting (2% of them an hour). Any deposit stops it.
  • Troops who survive a won siege come home a veteran star stronger.

The siege map

  • Click Siege at the Castle Ledger. The map grows out from Harrogath in rings: the further out, the harder the siege.
  • You can attack a tile that touches Harrogath or land you hold. Its terrain decides the battlefield, the enemy host and the warlord.
  • Fortresses hold a stronger host and a warlord whose spoils are doubled; every tenth ring is a warlord's capital.
  • A tile is yours when its warlord falls.

Laying siege

  • Pick a tile and Lay Siege. Put a squad of one troop line in each slot - the front row meets the enemy first - and fill every siege engine slot. Each line hunts its kind of enemy (spears the fast ones, archers the casters, Iron Wolves the armoured).
  • The bar shows how long your army holds the field without you. Your army can't win alone: it holds the line while you break the host.
  • Begin Siege and take the red portal. The enemy comes in waves, with champion packs.
  • The siege is lost if your army falls or every siege engine is destroyed - guard them. If you die, every troop you brought is lost.

The warlord

  • Win and a red portal opens to the warlord's camp. He grows tougher every ring out, with no limit. Kill him for his supplies (12 rolls of his terrain's supply and some of every other act's) and, rarely, a Siege Jewel: 1 in 71 on the first ring, 1 in 20 on ring 10, 1 in 9 on ring 25, at best 25% from ring 60 out - a fortress's warlord rolls twice.
  • Siege Jewels are rare jewels with one mod nothing else in the game rolls: +1 to +3 to any one skill of any class (210 skills). The deeper the ring, the bigger the bonus tends to be: near Harrogath +1 60%, +2 30%, +3 1 in 10; from ring 50 out +1 27%, +2 36%, +3 36%. Like any skill an item gives, +all skills raise it.

Together

  • The player who starts the siege commands it: only their army fights and only their tile is taken. Friends fight beside it, see the battle's banners, and each one there brings the warlord one more share of supplies.
  • Hardcore: the same rules - and a Hardcore commander's death takes the castle with it.
