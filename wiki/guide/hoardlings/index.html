Hoardlings

Hoardlings are glowing thieves - a beam of light marks them, a banner and a jingle announce them, and their name shows on the map (MapSense). Every map holds one, which shows up partway through the map - and a map with the of the Hoard or of Plunder affix holds two or three, one after another; in Hell's Torment and the rips each kill has a 1 in 4,000 chance to bring one. Only one is around at a time.

They don't fight: they run from the nearest player, and they are very tough. Every tenth of its life you take, a Hoardling drops some of its loot; kill it for the rest. 40 seconds after you first hit one it escapes with what it has left (one nobody touches leaves after 3 minutes).

Gilded (gold), Gem (perfect gems and Gem Clusters), Rune, Bag (Loot Bags), Cartographer (maps and map orbs), Soul (Soul Shards) and Jewel - the rarest (1 in 14 of them): mostly magic jewels, some rare, and a unique jewel on 17% of kills.
