Maps

Maps drop from monsters in Hell's Torment, Hell's terror zones and the rips - champions and unique packs most often. Put one in the Horadric Cube in Harrogath (Act 5 town, any difficulty) and Transmute: a red portal opens to its zone - Ruined Cathedral or Chaos Spire. The map is used up.

Three tiers: Tier 1 is monster level 110, Tier 2 118, Tier 3 125. Maps are always terrorized, so Heralds can appear. Map monsters are tougher (+75% life, +40% damage) and drop more maps: the same tier from regular monsters (1 in 1,900), the next tier from champions (1 in 190) and uniques (1 in 13).

Every map has a boss at the end: its maze has stairs down to the boss chamber - The Abbess in the Cathedral Sanctum (Ruined Cathedral), Seal Warden Kaeos on the Spire Throne (Chaos Spire). A boss rolls the Three's loot at half the chance of everything (5 item rolls on average, half their unique jewel, charm, Gem Cluster, Standard, Power Orb and Soul Shards), plus a map orb (always). Bosses don't drop maps: maps come from clearing the map, and the next tier from its champions and uniques or an Orb of Ascension. Map unique monsters roll 10 item picks each, champions and uniques roll the terror-zone elite tables, and every map has 14-20 unique and champion packs.

Every map is magic or rare (maps from before 1.1.34 stay plain). A magic map has a prefix and a suffix; a rare map rolls up to three of each, never two from the same pair, so it stacks dangers and rewards. A map's affixes apply to the whole run - the maze and the boss chamber - once you open it, and only while you're inside. Prefixes make the map harder and pay for it: Fortified (monsters +40% life, +8% experience); Bastion (monsters +80% life, +15% experience); Brutal (monsters +30% damage, +8% experience); Savage (monsters +60% damage, +15% experience); Frenzied (monsters +20% speed, +15% magic find); Rabid (monsters +40% speed, +30% magic find); Warded (monsters +15% resistances, +15% magic find); Impervious (monsters +30% resistances, +30% magic find); Teeming (+50% monsters, +10% experience); Swarming (+100% monsters, +20% experience); Legion (+50% champion and unique packs, +20% magic find); Horde (+100% champion and unique packs, +40% magic find). Suffixes are pure reward: of Fortune (+25% magic find); of Providence (+50% magic find); of Avarice (+60% gold); of Midas (+120% gold); of Insight (+10% experience); of Enlightenment (+20% experience); of the Hoard (+100% more Hoardlings); of Plunder (+200% more Hoardlings). The stronger affix of each pair needs item level 118: maps dropped in Tier 2 and 3 maps, deep in Hell's Torment or in the hardest rip. A map made in the cube (an orb or the 3-to-1 recipe) rolls new affixes.

3 maps of a tier and zone + Transmute = 1 map of the next tier of that zone.

Map orbs (cube one with a map, anywhere): Orb of Ascension = the same zone one tier up (Tier 1 and 2). Orb of Alteration = the other zone, same tier. Orb of Duplication = two of that map.

Map orbs drop inside maps (any monster 1 in 1,200, champions 1 in 120, unique monsters 1 in 12), in Hell's Torment (1 in 3,000; champions and uniques 1 in 300) and Hell terror zones (1 in 5,000; 1 in 500) - those two only Ascension and Alteration.

Keep maps in the Maps tab of your stash (after Runes): a 16x13 page that stays with the character, with slots for the map orbs on top (shared by all your characters, like runes). With the stash open on any tab, Ctrl+click (or Shift+click) sends a map straight to the Maps tab and the tab flashes; Ctrl+click moves maps and orbs back out. Dropped maps show a beam of light, and their names are coloured by tier: yellow, orange, red.

Loot Filter: maps have their own group under Items > Misc > Maps - tick it in a Show rule to always see them, or pick single maps.
