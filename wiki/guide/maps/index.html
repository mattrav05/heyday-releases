Guide · Endgame

Maps

Maps open endgame zones from the Horadric Cube: four zones, three tiers, magic and rare affixes and a boss at the end of each.

On this pageOpening a mapTiers and monstersThe bossAffixesUpgrading mapsStoring and spotting maps

Opening a map

  • Put a map in the Horadric Cube in Harrogath (Act 5 town, any difficulty) and Transmute: a red portal opens to its zone - Ruined Cathedral, Chaos Spire, Sunscorched Vaults or Bastion of Spite. The map is used up.
  • Maps drop from monsters in Hell's Torment, Hell's terror zones and the rips - champions and unique packs most often.
  • Sunscorched Vaults is one long, narrow tomb (Act 2's tiles); Bastion of Spite is a burning fortress of hate (Act 3's Durance tiles). Their maps - Sunscorched Vaults Map (Tier 1), Sunscorched Vaults Map (Tier 2), Sunscorched Vaults Map (Tier 3), Bastion of Spite Map (Tier 1), Bastion of Spite Map (Tier 2) and Bastion of Spite Map (Tier 3) - drop wherever maps drop, as often as the others.

Tiers and monsters

  • Three tiers: Tier 1 is monster level 110, Tier 2 118, Tier 3 125.
  • Maps are always terrorized, so Heralds can appear.
  • Map monsters are tougher (+75% life, +40% damage) and every map has 14-20 unique and champion packs.
  • More maps: the same tier from regular monsters (1 in 1,900), the next tier from champions (1 in 190) and uniques (1 in 13).
  • Map unique monsters roll 10 item picks each; champions and uniques roll the terror-zone elite tables.

The boss

Every map has a boss at the end.

  • Ruined Cathedral and Chaos Spire: the maze has stairs down to the boss chamber - The Abbess in the Cathedral Sanctum, Seal Warden Kaeos on the Spire Throne.
  • Sunscorched Vaults and Bastion of Spite: reaching the far end of the maze opens a red portal to the boss - The Sand Sovereign in the Tomb of the Sovereign, the Echo of Hatred in the Sanctum of Hatred.
  • Loot: a boss rolls the Three's loot at half the chance of everything (5 item rolls on average, half their unique jewel, charm, Gem Cluster, Standard, Power Orb and Soul Shards), plus a map orb (always).
  • Bosses don't drop maps: maps come from clearing the map, and the next tier from its champions and uniques or an Orb of Ascension.

Affixes

Every map is magic or rare (maps from before 1.1.34 stay plain). A magic map has a prefix and a suffix; a rare map rolls up to three of each, never two from the same pair, so it stacks dangers and rewards. Affixes apply to the whole run - the maze and the boss chamber - once you open it, and only while you're inside.

Prefixes make the map harder and pay for it:

  • Fortified: monsters +40% life, +40% magic find
  • Bastion: monsters +80% life, +75% magic find
  • Brutal: monsters +30% damage, +40% magic find
  • Savage: monsters +60% damage, +75% magic find
  • Frenzied: monsters +20% speed, +40% magic find
  • Rabid: monsters +40% speed, +75% magic find
  • Warded: monsters +15% resistances, +40% magic find
  • Impervious: monsters +30% resistances, +75% magic find
  • Teeming: +50% monsters, +40% magic find
  • Swarming: +100% monsters, +75% magic find
  • Legion: +50% champion and unique packs, +40% magic find
  • Horde: +100% champion and unique packs, +75% magic find
  • Hexed: players -20% all resistances, +40% magic find
  • Accursed: players -40% all resistances, +75% magic find
  • Armored: monsters 15% physical resistance, +40% magic find
  • Ironclad: monsters 30% physical resistance, +75% magic find
  • Frail: players -15% maximum life, +40% magic find
  • Withering: players -30% maximum life, +75% magic find
  • Vampiric: monsters steal 10% life, +40% magic find
  • Sanguine: monsters steal 20% life, +75% magic find
  • Hunted: +100% more Renegades, +40% magic find
  • Stalked: +200% more Renegades, +75% magic find

Suffixes are pure reward:

  • of Avarice: +60% gold
  • of Midas: +120% gold
  • of the Hoard: +100% more Hoardlings
  • of Plunder: +200% more Hoardlings
  • of Fortune: +40% magic find
  • of Providence: +75% magic find
  • of Abundance: +25% items from monsters (no maps)
  • of Bounty: +50% items from monsters (no maps)
  • of the Fallen: +100% Remnants
  • of the Restless Dead: +250% Remnants
  • of Insight: +40% experience
  • of Enlightenment: +80% experience
  • of Transcendence: +150% experience
  • of the Renegade: Renegades +50% better equipped
  • of the Warlord: Renegades +100% better equipped
  • Item level: the stronger affix of each pair needs item level 118 (maps dropped in Tier 2 and 3 maps, deep in Hell's Torment or in the hardest rip); of Transcendence needs item level 122 (maps dropped in Tier 3 maps or in the deepest levels of Hell's Torment).
  • A map made in the cube (an orb or the 3-to-1 recipe) rolls new affixes.
  • Experience in a map counts at Paragon too - at the max level it becomes Paragon experience.

Upgrading maps

  • 3 maps of a tier and zone + Transmute = 1 map of the next tier of that zone.
  • Orb of Ascension + map = the same zone one tier up (Tier 1 and 2).
  • Orb of Alteration + map = the next zone, same tier, in turn: Ruined Cathedral - Chaos Spire - Sunscorched Vaults - Bastion of Spite - and back to Ruined Cathedral.
  • Orb of Duplication + map = two of that map.
  • Map orbs work in the cube anywhere. They drop inside maps (any monster 1 in 1,200, champions 1 in 120, unique monsters 1 in 12), in Hell's Torment (1 in 3,000; champions and uniques 1 in 300) and in Hell terror zones (1 in 5,000; 1 in 500) - those two only Ascension and Alteration.

Storing and spotting maps

  • Keep maps in the Maps tab of your stash (after Runes): a 16x13 page that stays with the character, with slots for the map orbs on top (shared by all your characters, like runes).
  • With the stash open on any tab, Ctrl+click (or Shift+click) sends a map straight to the Maps tab and the tab flashes; Ctrl+click moves maps and orbs back out.
  • Dropped maps show a beam of light, and their names are coloured by tier: yellow, orange, red.
  • Loot Filter: maps have their own group under Items > Misc > Maps - tick it in a Show rule to always see them, or pick single maps.
