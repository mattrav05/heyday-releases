Colossal Summit

A secret area opened with the five Ancient Statues.

  • The five Ancient Statues - Talic's Anguish, Korlic's Pain, Madawc's Ire, Bul-Kathos' Nightmare and Worusk's End - drop rarely from terrorized Hell act bosses and Heralds.
  • Cube one of each to open the portal to the Colossal Summit.
  • A statue + the matching Worldstone Shard converts it into a different statue.
