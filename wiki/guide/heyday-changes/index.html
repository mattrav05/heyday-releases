Heyday Changes

Max character level 125, experience curve rebuilt for it. 6 stat points per level; 1 skill point per level and 2 on every 5th level (5, 10, 15...).

Legacy realm items are back: bugged Arkaine's Valor and Stormshield, Ith Crystal Sword, White Ring and Gloves, Wizzy gear, hacked amulets and more.

Every class is tuned up to the same level: each class's main mastery scales like the Sorceress's (+50% damage plus 12% per level, no cap), with Talonrage's stronger values wherever they still apply.

Uniques that also existed in Talonrage use the best of both versions.

Gambling: much better odds for uniques and sets.

Bosses can drop far more than 6 items (up to 32). Hell's Torment pays more the deeper you go, and the Maps the higher the tier: more extra rune rolls and better odds on elite gear.

Hell's Torment: 50 levels. It, the rips and the maps are always terrorized, with Heralds. The Talonrage rips (Hell 4-8) and the Guardian Angel are back.

Soul Summons: summon class followers from the Beacon of Hope (see the Heyday Guide tab).
