Upgrades

Runes: 2 of a rune + Tome of Town Portal = the next rune (Tome kept). 9 of El-Ral = the rune three steps up. Rune + Tome of Identify = the rune one step down. Cube a rune alone to start a stack.

Annihilus: + Jah + Ith + Ber unlocks tier 1, then 3 Orbs of Infusion, Assemblage, Corruption and Conversion for tiers 2-5 (+1% exp, +2 all stats, +2 all resistances each). At 5/5, 3 Orbs of Shadows turn it into the Black Soulstone.

Hellfire Torch: upgrade its tier with an Orb of Corruption and a Standard of Heroes (+1 to all skills per tier).

Unique jewels: 6 rare jewels = a unique jewel; Gem Bag (50 gems) + unique jewel = reroll it.
