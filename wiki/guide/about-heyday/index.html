About Heyday

HEYDAY - a free Diablo II: Resurrected mod. Not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.

Built on D2R Reimagined 3.0.12 by the Reimagined team (d2r-reimagined.com) - its items, recipes and systems are the foundation of Heyday.

Hell's Torment, the rips and many legacy uniques: Talonrage / Requiem of Sorrow 6.08.

Soul Summons followers: inspired by ReMoDDeD by locbones.

D2RLoader (d2rloader.net) - MapSense map plugin by RuffnecKk - drop limit hook location from SirCabby's D2RCabbyCodes.
