Loot Bags

Loot Bags drop in the maps (regular / champion / unique monsters: 1 in 150 / 1 in 15 / 33%), Hell's Torment (regular / champions and uniques: 1 in 500 / 1 in 50) and the rips (1 in 700 / 1 in 70). Put one in the Horadric Cube by itself and press Transmute: it opens and its loot falls at your feet. Bags stack in their own slot on the stash's Materials tab.

A bag rolls three times. Each roll can be nothing, junk gear, potions (including Experience, Magic Find and Sunder potions), a magic, rare or unique jewel, Soul Shards, Gem Clusters, Pandemonium Keys, a Standard of Heroes or a Hell's Torment key, an orb (not map orbs), runes, a unique ring or amulet, or an Ancient Statue - the better, the rarer. Per bag: a unique jewel 1 in 70, a unique ring or amulet 1 in 40, a high rune (Mal or better) 1 in 44, an Ancient Statue 1 in 80.
