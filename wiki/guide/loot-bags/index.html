Loot Bags

Bags of loot from the endgame. Cube one alone to open it.

On this pageWhere they dropOpening oneWhat's inside

Where they drop

  • Maps (regular / champion / unique monsters): 1 in 150 / 1 in 15 / 33%.
  • Hell's Torment (regular / champions and uniques): 1 in 500 / 1 in 50.
  • The rips: 1 in 700 / 1 in 70.

Opening one

  • Put one in the Horadric Cube by itself and press Transmute: it opens and its loot falls at your feet.
  • Bags stack in their own slot on the stash's Materials tab.

What's inside

A bag rolls three times. Each roll can be nothing, junk gear, potions (including Experience, Magic Find and Sunder potions), a magic, rare or unique jewel, Soul Shards, Gem Clusters, Pandemonium Keys, a Standard of Heroes or a Hell's Torment key, an orb (not map orbs), runes, a unique ring or amulet, or an Ancient Statue - the better, the rarer.

  • Unique jewel: 1 in 70 per bag.
  • Unique ring or amulet: 1 in 40 per bag.
  • High rune (Mal or better): 1 in 44 per bag.
  • Ancient Statue: 1 in 80 per bag.
