The league - how it stays fair

1. You sign in. Sign in with Discord in the launcher proves who you are (Heyday never sees your password) and ties the league to your Discord account.

2. The vault hands you your characters. Before the game starts, the launcher downloads your league characters and stash from the league server. The vault's copy is the official one.

3. The game is checked before it starts. League mode only runs the official, unmodified Heyday files and plugins, and switches the developer console off.

4. A witness rides along. The league plugin records what you carry when you enter a game, every item that arrives after that (with the drop it came from), your kills by area, and how the game was started. Each line is signed with a one-time key for this session, so the log can't be edited, trimmed or reordered.

5. Everything goes back to the vault. While you play (every minute) and when you close the game, the launcher sends the log and your saves to the server.

6. The server checks it all. It verifies the signed log, then checks every save: level vs experience, stat and skill points, gold, and every item's stats against Heyday's own data. Each item has a fingerprint; a new one must match a witnessed arrival, and a rare or better must match a real drop at its item level and area. Trades are followed from player to player; the same item on two characters means a dupe.

7. Bad uploads bounce. An impossible save is refused, and the vault keeps your last good copy - nothing is lost, and old copies can't be restored to undo a death or a bad trade.

8. Statistics watch the long run. Luck far beyond the league's own drop rates, bot-like pace and marathon sessions are flagged.

9. People decide. Flags go to the moderators, who can look at a character's full history, roll back items or ban an account. Notable finds are posted in #finds, so everyone sees the real loot.
