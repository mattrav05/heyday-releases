Start Here

Heyday is Diablo II: Resurrected with a long endgame on top. The campaign plays like the game you know; the new things start in Hell and keep going long after level 125.

On this pageYour road through HeydayAlong the wayPlaying with friends

Your road through Heyday

  • Normal to Hell: play the campaign. Your first Normal Baal kill drops the Beacon of Hope for Soul Summons, your followers.
  • Hell Baal and the Spirits: Baal and the four Spirits (Andariel, Duriel, Diablo, Mephisto) drop Soul Shards; Baal and the Spirits of Diablo and Mephisto drop Hell's Torment Portal Keys.
  • Hell's Torment: 50 levels straight down, always terrorized, monster level 110 to 125. Maps start dropping here.
  • Maps: cube one in Harrogath to open its zone. Three tiers, magic and rare affixes, a boss at the end.
  • The Rips: kill the Guardian Angel on the Arreat Summit in Hell for the key. Monster level 98, 103, 108, 113, 118.
  • Paragon: at level 125 your experience keeps counting, as Paragon points for more loot.

Along the way

  • Renegades: rival heroes who hunt you anywhere outside town. Run or fight - their Spoils are worth it.
  • Hoardlings and Remnants: loot thieves and gifts of the fallen in the endgame areas.
  • Runner Contracts: hire runners in the Barracks and send them to run your maps for you.
  • Loot Bags, map orbs and the upgrade recipes turn spare loot into progress.

Playing with friends

Multiplayer is built in - see Playing Together. The league (on the Heyday website) checks every drop and every character.
