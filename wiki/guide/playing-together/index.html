Playing Together

Pick an offline character and press Multiplayer next to Play. Host: Public lists your game in the game browser, Unlisted gives you a Game ID to share, LAN is for your own network. Join: Browse Games, or enter the host's Game ID or connection details.

Hosting uses TCP port 4000. D2RLoader sets it up automatically and shows whether others can reach you; some routers need port 4000 forwarded by hand.

Everyone needs the same Heyday and D2RLoader version. The Heyday launcher keeps both matched, and D2RLoader checks before anyone joins.
