Hell's Torment

Cube a Hell's Torment Portal Key with a Tome of Town Portal to open the Antechamber. From there it is 50 levels straight down - no waypoints, no checkpoints, and teleporting works like in Duriel's Lair - only where you could walk, never over or through walls. Every level has an Icechamber side area.

Monsters go from level 110 to 125. The whole dungeon is always terrorized, so Heralds can appear on any level.

Loot improves with depth (monster level 110, 114, 118, 122, 125): every monster rolls an extra rune (any rune up to Zod) and elite gear with raised unique odds, and both get likelier the deeper you go - champions and uniques far more than regular monsters. Maps: regular monsters 1 in 10,829, champions and uniques 1 in 1,820. Map orbs: 1 in 3,000 and 1 in 300.

At the bottom wait the Three. Their chamber is always terrorized, so every kill rolls their terror-zone table: 10 item rolls at boosted unique and set odds; unique jewel: 26%; charm with strongly boosted unique odds: always (unique chance per kill: 1 in 12; class Hellfire Torches included); Gem Cluster: always; Standard of Heroes: 25%; Power Orb: always (Diablo: Conversion, Mephisto: Assemblage, Baal: Corruption); about 2.5 Soul Shards; plus the boss and terror-zone bonus rolls (Sunder Charms included).
