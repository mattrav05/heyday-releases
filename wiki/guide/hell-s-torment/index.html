Hell's Torment

Fifty levels straight down, always terrorized - and the deeper you go, the better the loot.

On this pageGetting inMonstersIcechamber bossesLoot by depthThe Three

Getting in

  • Cube a Hell's Torment Portal Key with a Tome of Town Portal to open the Antechamber.
  • Keys drop from Baal and from the Spirits of Diablo and Mephisto: 1 in 24.
  • From the Antechamber it is 50 levels straight down: no waypoints, no checkpoints.
  • Teleport works like in Duriel's Lair - only where you could walk, never over or through walls.

Monsters

  • Monster level 110 at the top, 125 at the bottom.
  • Always terrorized, so Heralds can appear on any level.

Icechamber bosses

Every level has an Icechamber side room, and in each one waits a boss - big, tough and alone, behind the Wardens of the Frozen Gate who hold its entrance.

  • Five bosses take turns down the levels: Ender'Aku, Majiko, Raku'Ranth, Kelthir and Grizzlepaw.
  • Every tenth level holds a greater one: Izual the Forsaken (10), Ded'Domer (20), Gurka (30), Queen Syndred (40) and Anuburak (50).
  • They resist every element and shrug off part of your physical and magic damage, but none is immune.
  • They drop Hell's Torment's own elite loot, better the deeper you go; the greater ones drop more of it.

Loot by depth

Loot steps up at monster level 110, 114, 118, 122, 125.

  • Every monster rolls an extra rune (any rune up to Zod) and elite gear with raised unique odds. Both get likelier the deeper you go - for champions and uniques far more than for regular monsters.
  • Maps: regular monsters 1 in 10,829, champions and uniques 1 in 1,820.
  • Map orbs: regular monsters 1 in 3,000, champions and uniques 1 in 300.

The Three

At the bottom wait the Three. Their chamber is always terrorized, so every kill rolls their terror-zone table:

  • Items: 10 item rolls at boosted unique and set odds.
  • Unique jewel: 26%.
  • Charm: always, with strongly boosted unique odds (unique chance per kill: 1 in 12; class Hellfire Torches included).
  • Gem Cluster: always. Standard of Heroes: 25%.
  • Power Orb: always (Diablo: Conversion, Mephisto: Assemblage, Baal: Corruption).
  • Soul Shards: about 2.5.
  • Plus the boss and terror-zone bonus rolls (Sunder Charms included).
