The Barracks (new)

Runner Contracts are a new drop, rare and found where maps drop: any monster in Hell's Torment (1 in 10,829 / 1 in 1,820 per kill for a regular monster / a champion or unique), in the rips (1 in 30,485 / 1 in 3,731), in Hell's terror zones (1 in 30,485 / 1 in 3,731) and inside maps (1 in 10,829 / 1 in 1,820), and map bosses (1 in 150). Each contract is a runner with a build: 41% of them are the generic Sellsword Contract (Sellsword: fights wherever the team needs. Wants a weapon, armor, Life. No skills), each class build 1 in 12 to 1 in 16:

Vanguard's Contract (Barbarian, Build: Warcry Barbarian) - Tank: draws the hits. Wants Defense, Block, Life. Skills: Battle Orders, Shout, Battle Command; Iron Skin, Natural Resistance, Whirlwind.

Templar's Contract (Paladin, Build: Fanaticism Zealot) - Aura warden: covers the team. Wants Resistances, Life, Block. Skills: Zeal, Fanaticism and one aura: Holy Freeze or Conviction or Salvation; Sacrifice, Vigor.

Pyromancer's Contract (Sorceress, Build: Meteor Sorceress) - Fire caster: Wants +Skills, Faster Cast Rate. Skills: Meteor, Fire Ball, Fire Mastery; Warmth, Teleport.

Frost Witch's Contract (Sorceress, Build: Blizzard Sorceress) - Cold caster: Wants +Skills, Faster Cast Rate. Skills: Blizzard, Frozen Orb, Ice Blast, Cold Mastery; Teleport.

Bowmaiden's Contract (Amazon, Build: Multishot Amazon) - Archer: Wants a bow, Enhanced Damage, Attack Speed. Skills: Multiple Shot, Strafe, Penetrate, Critical Strike; Valkyrie.

Bonecaller's Contract (Necromancer, Build: Skeleton Necromancer) - Summoner and curser: Wants +Skills, Life, Resistances. Skills: Raise Skeleton, Skeleton Mastery; Amplify Damage, Lower Resist, Bone Armor.

Stormcaller's Contract (Druid, Build: Wind Druid) - Storm warden: shields and slows. Wants +Skills, Resistances, Life. Skills: Hurricane, Tornado, Twister; Cyclone Armor, Oak Sage.

Shadow's Contract (Assassin, Build: Trap Assassin) - Trapper: Wants +Skills, Faster Cast Rate, Life. Skills: Lightning Sentry, Death Sentry, Shock Web; Fade, Burst of Speed.

Every contract has base stats that grow with its item level - for a Sellsword from Runner Life 600-800, Runner Defense 800-1200, Runner Damage 60-90 up to Life 1200-1600, Defense 2000-3000, Damage 160-240 from item level 124. Tanks have more life and defense, casters and archers more damage. Every contract carries its whole build, like a character's skill tree: main skills and synergies at 10-14 (up to 16-20 from item level 124), utility skills at 1-4 (up to 6-10). Magic and rare contracts add more on top: build suffixes give +1-5 to one of the build's skills each, and a rare can have three. The skills are the runner's, not yours: a contract does nothing for the player.

Contracts are magic, rare or unique. Magic and rare contracts roll traits: prefixes make the runner better at a role - Vanguard: Steadfast 5-15%, Ironwall 15-30%, Unbroken 30-50%; Striker: Keen 5-15%, Ruthless 15-30%, Bladesworn 30-50%; Warden: Vigilant 5-15%, Runebound 15-30%, Sanctified 30-50%. Suffixes help the whole team - survival: of Grit 5-15%, of Endurance 15-30%, of the Undying 30-50%; loot found: of Scavenging 5-15%, of Pillage 15-30%, of the Hoard-Keeper 30-50%. The stronger traits and skill suffixes need item level 115 and 122: contracts from Tier 2 and Tier 3 maps and deep Hell's Torment. Rare contracts can carry several skills and traits at once. 10 legendary runners are unique contracts (item level 110-124) with fixed strong stats, their build's skills at high levels and big traits:Kashya's Captain (Bowmaiden), Gheed's Debt Collector (Sellsword), Larzuk's Shieldbearer (Vanguard), The Last Horadrim (Pyromancer), Ormus's Apprentice (Frost Witch), Asheara's Blade (Shadow), Hratli's Ward (Templar), Drognan's Bonecaller (Bonecaller), Malah's Storm (Stormcaller), Qual-Kehk's Veteran (Vanguard).

Take contracts to the Barracks in Harrogath (Act 5). The Barracks is a building in Harrogath (Act 5 town): the stone farmhouse in the south-east yard, past Anya and the campfire, by the town's south wall. Walk in through its open front and click the War Table inside to hire a contract's runner for gold (a hired runner keeps the contract: hover it in its slot to see its stats), gear them, and send up to four runners to run one of your maps without you: Vanguards take the hits, Strikers clear, Wardens cover the map's elements and curses. A run takes 15 / 25 / 40 minutes of play for a Tier 1 / 2 / 3 map, 3 more for each dangerous affix it carries - counted only while that character is in a game. What the map would have dropped lands in the run's loot tab; runners who die are gone, with everything they wore.
