About Heyday

HEYDAY - a free Diablo II: Resurrected mod. Not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.

Endgame

Hell's Torment

Fifty levels straight down, always terrorized - and the deeper you go, the better the loot.

Spirits & Baal

The Spirits are stronger echoes of the act bosses, and with Baal they are your way into Hell's Torment.

Maps

Maps open endgame zones from the Horadric Cube: four zones, three tiers, magic and rare affixes and a boss at the end...

The Rips (Hell 4-8)

Five rips past Hell, each harder than the last, opened with a rune.

Colossal Summit

A secret area opened with the five Ancient Statues.

Encounters

Renegades

Rival heroes gone bad who hunt players. Very tough - often the right call is to run - but their Spoils are worth the...

Hoardlings

Glowing loot thieves: they run, you chase, and every hit shakes loot loose.

Remnants

Sometimes the fallen leave something behind: a shrine-like stone with a gift for whoever touches it first.

Your character

Paragon

At level 125 your experience keeps counting - as Paragon levels, forever. Every point is Fortune: more loot, never...

Soul Summons (Followers)

Class followers you summon from the Beacon of Hope and level up with Soul Shards and Class Tokens.

The Barracks

Hire runners from Runner Contracts and send them to run your maps without you.

Loot & crafting

Loot Bags

Bags of loot from the endgame. Cube one alone to open it.

Upgrades

Cube recipes that turn spare runes, orbs and jewels into progress.
