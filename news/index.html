Patch notes

Every release, newest first. The launcher installs updates for you.

2026-11-06

1.1.62: Renegades match the area

Renegades match the area. Renegades were tuned against endgame characters and hit just as hard everywhere - in early areas they had far too much life, healed too fast and simply killed lower-level characters. Now they scale with the area they turn up in: early on a Renegade has much less life and regeneration, hits softer, casts, leaps and summons more slowly, its skills grow more slowly and its gear (speed, resistances, +skills and the rest) counts for less. Deep in Hell - Torment, the Rips, the Maps and the toughest terror zones - they are unchanged. More players in the game still means more life, as for every monster.

2026-11-06

1.1.61: Renegades raise the dead, and multiplayer catches up

Renegades bring help. The Bonecaller raises skeletons and the Shadow plants lightning sentries around you. The Templar's Zeal gets faster as its skill grows. The Pyromancer, Frost Witch and Bonecaller now hold their orb or wand while they cast, and the Bowmaiden finally draws her bow - and uses her skills, which she never did before.

Fewer, but they mean it. Every Renegade got a balance pass against a real endgame character, so each one is a fight. They also turn up much less often: it's pure chance on each kill, raised by map affixes and the Paragon Renegades stat.

Multiplayer. If you join someone else's game, you now see the Renegade warning and fallen banners and the Hoardling and Remnant messages - they never reached joined players before. Joined players' Paragon, their Renegade spoils and their own Paragon bonuses on Hoardlings and Remnants were tested in a real two-player game and work.

2026-11-06

1.1.60: Renegades fixed and fiercer, Spoils you can come back to, a bigger Sunscorched Vaults

Renegades fixed. You can now click Renegades and see their health bar, they fight instead of standing still or running in place, and your hits stick - they no longer heal faster than you can hurt them. The warning before one arrives is now a bright banner with a heartbeat you can actually hear.

Fiercer. Renegades hit much harder (real elite-weapon blows for the Vanguard, Templar and Bowmaiden), have more life, and their skills level faster with them. Their gear can now be unique or set, not just rare - it shows unidentified, like any drop.

Spoils you can come back to. The Spoils screen has an X. Close it (or press Escape) with nothing taken and nothing is lost: come back to the Renegade's corpse and its Spoils open again. The runewords a Renegade carries no longer fill your Chronicle the moment it appears - only what you actually take counts.

Sunscorched Vaults. The maze is four times as big, and its monsters now match the other map zones: the same resistances, life and experience (they gave about a tenth of the experience before). The same fix applies to the Bastion of Spite's monsters.

2026-11-05

1.1.59: Renegades, their Spoils, map zone names, Paragon pages

Renegades (new). Rival heroes gone bad now hunt players: a Vanguard, Templar, Pyromancer, Frost Witch, Bowmaiden, Bonecaller, Stormcaller or Shadow. Any kill outside town can call one (rare - about as often as a Herald). First a warning - "You are being hunted..." and a heartbeat - then it arrives a few seconds later, a little way off: stand your ground or flee. They look and move like the real class, carry real weapons, cast their contract build's skills at a level that grows with theirs, have their build's masteries - and the gear they wear powers them while they live. Very tough by design.

Spoils. Kill one and its Spoils open beside your inventory: its gear laid out like a character's, and its pockets (charms, orbs, shards, gem clusters, potions, scrolls and whatever it found in the maps - never gear). Select ONE piece of gear, or its whole pockets: what you select drops at your feet, the rest is gone. Its gear suits its class and gets better with its level; its weapon, off-hand, armor or helm can be a runeword in a good or a bad base (high-level runewords are the rarest), and it sometimes carries its own Runner Contract.

Map affixes. Hunted and Stalked bring more Renegades; of the Renegade and of the Warlord make them better equipped - more runewords, fuller pockets, and contracts more often.

Paragon. A new Renegades stat (more Renegades on your kills) and a Renegades slain counter. The page holds any number of stats and counters: when they outgrow it, arrows beside Reset turn both lists together. The + buttons are smaller, and the HUD's Paragon button is back above the skill bar - it hides while your inventory or skill tree is open.

Map zone names. The two newest map zones have names of their own: Tombs of the Sands is now the Sunscorched Vaults and Durance of Hate is the Bastion of Spite. Maps you already have are renamed with them; nothing else changes.

2026-11-05

1.1.58: Paragon button, new map zone loot, Remnant fixes

Paragon button. The Paragon button no longer sits over your inventory: it now shows in the middle, above the level-up buttons, where no window covers it. Opening the Paragon page dismisses it until your next Paragon level, even if you keep your points for later.

Tombs of the Sands and Durance of Hate loot. Their monsters were rolling their home act's Hell loot tables - a little worse than the other map zones - and their champions and uniques missed the terror elite tables every other map has. They now drop exactly like Ruined Cathedral and Chaos Spire monsters of the same tier.

Remnants. Remnants could be forgotten and then do nothing when touched - most likely in hosted Multiplayer games. They now stay tracked, and in Multiplayer a Remnant's gift goes to the player nearest it, as intended.

2026-11-04

1.1.57: Two new map zones, big experience maps, Paragon reset

Two new map zones. Tombs of the Sands (Act 2's tombs) and Durance of Hate (Mephisto's halls) join Ruined Cathedral and Chaos Spire, each with three tiers and its own maps - they drop wherever maps drop, as often as the others. Reach the far end of the maze and a red portal opens to the boss: The Sand Sovereign in the Tomb of the Sovereign, the Echo of Hatred in the Sanctum of Hatred. The Orb of Alteration now turns a map into the next zone in turn (Ruined Cathedral - Chaos Spire - Tombs of the Sands - Durance of Hate - and back).

Big experience maps for Paragon. A map's experience bonus counts as Paragon experience at the max level, so the experience suffixes are much stronger now: of Insight +40% (was +10%), of Enlightenment +80% (was +20%), and the new of Transcendence +150% on Tier 3 maps. Maps you already have keep their rolls.

Paragon. Each new Paragon level is announced on screen, a Paragon button shows above the skill bar while you have points to spend, and the Paragon page has a Reset button (click it twice) that gives every point back, free. In a multiplayer game, a summon's or mercenary's kill now counts for the player nearest it, as intended - it went to the first player in the game.

Crash fixes. The most common crash on 1.1.54-1.1.56 is fixed: the Paragon page and the Barracks panel could crash the game when it had moved their text. Buff HUD could crash the game later after the HUD was rebuilt (switching between controller and mouse, for example).

Controller players: Buff HUD no longer shows a wall of empty buff slots after switching to a controller (thanks to Pyre-Flies' fix to Buff HUD).

2026-11-03

1.1.56: Buff timers, a Multiplayer crash fix and Remnants of War

Crash fix: ending a hosted Multiplayer game. The game could crash when a hosted game ended - often the first Multiplayer game after starting the game. One of Heyday's lookups could read the game while it was being closed; Heyday now does that lookup in a way that can't crash.

Remnants of War always answer. A Remnant left by a monster that explodes when it appears (like Baal's suicide minions) raised more of them, which blew up at once - it looked like nothing happened. Those Remnants now raise an ordinary monster of the area instead, and if a War spawn ever leaves nothing near you, you get a Gauntlet, a Hoardling or Fortune's blessing instead.

Buff timers. Your buffs now show as icons with a countdown just above the skill bar - Battle Orders, Shout, armors, shrines and more - thanks to MindH1ve's Buff HUD plugin, which now comes with Heyday. The Remnants' gifts are there too: Find Item's icon for the +100% magic find, Find Potion's for the +200% gold find, and War Cry's for a Gauntlet in progress.

2026-11-03

1.1.55: Joining games, Oil Lamp recipes, a smoother Paragon page

Joining other players' games. Joining a hosted Heyday game could fail with "Game data does not match" when your D2RLoader settings differed from the host's (the Aura Enchanted and Bind Demon rules, or the stat and skill reset shortcut). Heyday now sets these for every player, so they always match. The Alt+click reset shortcut is off in Heyday; respecs work the usual way. If a join still fails with "Excel tables" in red, one of you is on an older version of D2R - update the game through Steam or Battle.net and try again.

Oil Lamp cube recipes. The Oil Lamp now works in every amulet recipe: the Orbs of Conversion, Assemblage, Infusion and Corruption, the enchants, crafts, rerolls and recycling. Conversion and Assemblage turn it into a unique or set Amulet; enchants, Infusion and the three-lamp reroll keep it an Oil Lamp.

The Paragon page opens smoothly. The first time you opened it in a game, the page froze for a few seconds and showed placeholder text. It now fills in almost at once.

2026-11-02

1.1.54: Paragon, and Hoardlings, Remnants and Contracts in terror zones

Paragon: levelling never stops. At level 125 your experience keeps counting as Paragon experience, forever. Every Paragon level gives one point, and each level takes longer than the last. Open the new Paragon page from the button under Breakpoints on the character screen (it appears at level 125) and click + to spend points (Shift: 10).

Fortune only, no caps. Paragon points go into Magic Find, Gold Find, Hoardling chance, Remnant chance and Contract chance - more loot, never more power. Nothing is capped, but every stat has diminishing returns: the page shows what you put in and, in brackets, what you actually get. It also keeps your lifetime Paragon experience, Hoardlings caught, Remnants touched and bonus contracts.

Your Paragon is yours in Multiplayer too. When you join someone's game, your bonuses come with you and the Paragon experience you earn there is yours. Your Hoardling, Remnant and Contract chance apply to your own kills.

Terror zones are worth more. Hoardlings, Remnants and Runner Contracts now appear in Hell's terror zones, at the same odds as the rips.

2026-10-31

1.1.50: The Barracks - hire runners to run your maps

The Barracks is open. Hire runners to run your maps for you. A new building stands in Harrogath's south-east yard, past Anya and the campfire - look for the red cross and the "Barracks" label on the map. Inside is the War Table.

Runner Contracts are a new drop, about as rare as maps, found where maps drop: Hell's Torment, the rips, inside maps and from map bosses. Every contract is a runner with a build - the generic Sellsword or one of 8 class builds (Warcry Barbarian, Zealot, Meteor and Blizzard Sorceress, Multishot Amazon, Skeleton Necromancer, Wind Druid, Trap Assassin). Magic and rare contracts roll traits (tank, damage or element roles, survival, more loot) and extra skill levels; 10 legendary runners are unique contracts.

How it works: take a contract to the War Table, hire its runner for gold and gear them like a character - their gear slots fill in once they're hired. Put a map on the table and send up to four runners. The success bar shows the odds (hover for the %); only very good gear gets a team past 90%. A run takes a while - longer for higher tier maps and dangerous affixes - and only counts down while that character is in a game.

Runs and deaths: a failed run usually means a retreat, not a funeral. A runner can die and the map still succeed, and the whole team rarely falls - better gear means fewer deaths. A runner who dies is gone with everything they wore. Whatever the map would have dropped lands in the run's loot tab; empty it before the next run.

The Heyday Guide (Mod Information) and the wiki have the full details: every build, trait, unique runner and drop rate.

2026-10-25

1.1.48: Remnants, stronger Soul Summons and chance-to-cast fixes

Remnants. In Hell's Torment, the maps and the rips the fallen sometimes leave something behind: a Remnant, a shrine-like stone where the monster died. Touch it and:

- Remnant of Fortune: +100% magic find or +200% gold find for 2 minutes, or loot spills out - Abundance (a top-tier map unique's loot, twice), 3 runes or 4 perfect gems, skulls and gem clusters.

- Remnant of War: a champion of the fallen rises, a Herald of Terror answers with its group, or the Gauntlet begins - 3 waves of 8 of the fallen's kind around you. Clear them all within 45 seconds for both Fortune gifts and a Hoardling.

- Remnant of Greed: a Hoardling appears.

New map suffixes of the Fallen and of the Restless Dead leave many more Remnants. Everything is in the Heyday Guide.

Soul Summons followers. Followers now gain 3 skill levels per tier, so a tier 10 follower casts at level 30 (was 20). High-tier followers were missing gear: tier 9-10 followers now wear unique armor (and Paladins, Barbarians and Assassins unique weapons) and the Sorceress follower has gloves. The tier 1 Sorceress follower no longer casts Meteor at level 20. The guide and wiki now list every follower's skills.

Chance-to-cast fixes. Bone Prison (Arm of King Leoric, Skullcollector), Bone Wall (of Bone Walls), Thunder Storm (Thunder, Punishment) and the of Hurricane / of Armageddon / of Blade Shield / of Korlics Fury suffixes now actually cast - they did nothing and logged a game error before.

Hoardlings are back on the latest Steam version. After Steam's D2R update, Hoardlings and the Teeming / Swarming map affixes switched themselves off - they work again.

2026-10-19

1.1.40: Crash reports

Crash reports. When a Heyday game crashes or logs an error, the launcher now sends a report to Heyday by itself, so problems get fixed without anyone having to post logs in Discord - especially on Battle.net, where your game version can differ from the one Heyday is tested on. A report holds the version numbers, the game's crash report text and Heyday's own error lines and plugin logs. Your Windows user name, PC name and folder paths are taken out first, and nothing is sent about games that went fine. Don't want it? Untick Send crash reports in the launcher. (Launcher 1.2.14 - it updates itself.)

Battle.net players: features are back. 1.1.38 switched map affixes, Hoardlings, Loot Bags opening, Drop Limit and the multiplayer boss-life fix off on any game version other than Steam's, to stop the crashes. Crash fix for some players (Battle.net). Several of Heyday's features use the game's own code directly, and they were built for one game version. Bosses no longer break in big games. 2026-10-18

1.1.32: Hoardlings

Hoardlings: glowing loot thieves. A map has a one-in-three chance to hold one, and they turn up now and then in Hell's Torment and the rips - a banner and a coin jingle tell you when one is near. They don't fight back: they run from you, blink away when cornered, and are very tough. Every tenth of its life you take, a Hoardling drops some of its loot; kill it for the big haul. Be quick - 40 seconds after your first hit it escapes with whatever is left.

Seven kinds: Gilded (gold), Gem (perfect gems and Gem Clusters), Rune, Bag (Loot Bags), Cartographer (maps and map orbs), Soul (Soul Shards) and the rare Jewel Hoardling (magic and rare jewels, and a chance at a unique jewel when you kill it). Their names show in gold, and MapSense marks them on your map.

MapSense names bosses correctly. Map bosses and other added superuniques no longer show another boss's name on the map.

Simplified Chinese now uses the Traditional Chinese font family for the main UI font, so both Chinese languages match - thanks to Solaris for the suggestion.

The download is a little smaller: Heyday no longer ships unchanged copies of the game's own files.

You can now delete a league character for good.
